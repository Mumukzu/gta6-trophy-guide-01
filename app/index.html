Road to the platinum

Earn them in this order. Stage 1 is the story; stage 2 is the things to grab while you play; stages 3 and 4 are the grind; stage 5 is the robberies, then 100%, then the platinum.

    Don’t miss this one

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida. Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 counts toward it and disappears after that chapter, and there is no mission replay. Meditate at the balcony in Chapter 1, then at every other spot as you find them. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, and one more before the final mission. Source: Output Lag — trophy list, also flagged by EGamers (GTABase / PowerPyx).
